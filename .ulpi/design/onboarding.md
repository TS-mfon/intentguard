Every screen binds to DESIGN.md. Design Read: issue a clear spending permission, then give the owner a physical card they can inspect and control. Retain industrial/signage product surfaces and the scoped smoked-glass issued card. Use existing Radix primitives, graphite/sage tokens, IBM Plex families, split-slot mark, and 48px controls.

## Accepted product contract

New setup defaults to Quick setup: Choose template → Customize → Review and activate. Keep the manual Identity → Permissions → Money & validity → Review editor. Both modes share the exact form, serializer, validation, draft polling and activation/recovery engine. Switching modes never discards data. Existing saved four-step drafts restore in manual mode; new forms start in quick mode. Preserve original failed proposal input and transaction history. No consensus failure or clarification can authorize activation.

Research assistant, API access and Protocol test supply every policy section: name/mission/context, categories/exclusions/exceptions, merchants/products/origins, evidence/freshness/criteria, asset/destinations, budgets/period and UTC validity. Keep existing small starting amounts (1 lifetime, 0.01/payment, 0.1/day), one-day evidence freshness and 30-day validity, all editable. Exceptions explicitly show None. Research/API example merchant identifiers are clearly marked for replacement; do not represent a guessed wallet as verified. Use a non-repeated-digit illustrative address and merchant.example origin as example-only values; validation blocks example identifiers before proposal. Protocol test takes its actual public merchant profile from config.setupMerchant, on testnet1952 only. It must not use a guessed affiliate or expose an issuer credential. A unavailable public merchant profile offers a complete example with an actionable missing-details state. No fabricated live readiness.

Quick Customize has two small essential groups: identity (name, mission, project/context) and merchant (name, recipient, HTTPS origin). Show the current asset, networks, budgets, period and expiry in a short editable summary. Full policy controls remain in expandable groups with at most four related fields per group. Applying a merchant origin also initializes its evidence origin; preserve explicitly edited additional evidence origins. Defaults only change when a template is explicitly applied. Never rewrite numeric limits into mission/criteria. Say that invoice ID, amount and buyer vault are supplied and checked per later request, and that supporting evidence sources do not expand the merchant allowlist.

Use a brief title and one sentence per setup view. Remove duplicate page/sidebar/section explanations. Keep help beside a focused field or in disclosure. Exactly one primary forward action at a time. Detailed identifiers, raw policy, diagnostic codes and receipts are available under Details. Final review must expose all exact permission fields and require explicit owner acceptance before the wallet transaction. After activation auto-register the confirmed receipt and open that agent's detail view; show reviewer initialization progress honestly. Funding and one-time key issuance remain explicit owner actions.

## States and recovery

Preparing form, validating, submitted, waiting for consensus, clarification, consensus failure, successfully finalized proposal, wallet request, confirmed activation/registering, reviewer initializing, ready, offline and unavailable each have distinct status and recovery action. Polling is cancellable; transient outages resume the existing draft/activation transaction rather than create a duplicate. Terminal clarification stops the spinner and offers Edit inputs. Validation focuses the first relevant field and opens its collapsed group. Consensus/provider diagnostics remain subordinate to plain messages. Do not promise that a template guarantees consensus.

Autosave only form/mode/step/template and the admitted draft ID, scoped to API/payment environment/chain/owner. Never persist owner tokens, wallet signatures or agent keys. Refresh, back/forward, session expiry and account changes preserve correctly scoped drafts. Exact restored policy dates retain seconds. Wrong owner/environment identifiers never load another owner's form. Wallet rejection retains the finalized policy and allows another explicit activation attempt. An uncertain confirmed activation reconciles its saved receipt before replay.

## Accessibility and visual acceptance

320,390,768 and1440px layouts have no horizontal overflow. Use existing contrast roles (primary15.8:1 and muted9.3:1 on product background; card secondary≥5.38:1 at its allowed brightest composed background), visible focus and 48px targets. Use semantic labels/links/buttons, appropriate aria-busy/live messages, keyboard-complete disclosures and tab behavior. Reduced motion is static. No new palette, typography, generic dashboard glass, fake usage or animated financial values. Do not add external fonts or image dependencies.

Design preflight: locked tokens only; physical issued card is the signature; purposeful form/gallery/detail layout families; groups of at most four fields; one primary action; all error/loading/empty/offline/session/wallet states specified.
