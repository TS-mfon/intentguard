# Why we exist: an animated spending journey
Binds to DESIGN.md. The existing landing, explorers, docs and owner UI stay visually unchanged. Add /why-we-exist, a fifth navigation item, and route-scoped styling only.
## Design read
The issued ATM identity becomes a working machine: an agent presents its card, a request crosses two distinct gates, and only then does a merchant receive funds. Industrial/signage remains locked. The drama comes from mechanical action and information, never neon fog or generic dashboard cards.
## Experience and story
Opening: oversized WHY WE EXIST display with a full-width cinematic agent transacting with its engraved IntentGuard ATM card. Show request formation, card tap/reader action, permission check, evidence review, and tokens arriving at a merchant. Use a 24–32 second multi-scene film, not an idle floating-card loop. The film has original synchronized sound (card swipe, mechanism, computation, confirmation plus restrained score), local fonts, a poster and explicit video pause/sound/replay controls.
Normal motion may autoplay muted; Play story with sound is a deliberate gesture. Sound is never enabled unexpectedly. Sound preference stays local. Pause/tab leave/offscreen suspend playback and audio. Volume controls and muted state are visibly discoverable. Failed loading shows poster and retry; slow loading never leaves a blank hero.
Scroll continuation: native progressive scrolling moves a sticky mechanical stage through chapters. A request path fills, the card travels and its gate state changes as text resolves. Every substantial visual transforms with scroll progress, not only a one-time fade. Large editorial statement, sideways circuit view, asymmetric evidence assembly and final merchant settlement are distinct layouts. Do not hijack wheel, touch, keyboard or browser scroll.
Chapters: (1) An instruction can become an expensive mistake, attributed reported Lobstar $250K holdings incident, no unverified 4 SOL detail. (2) Human intent becomes an immutable mandate. (3) TapeOut circuit on X Layer blocks numeric/recipient/asset/enabled violations before review; a blocked branch visibly skips GenLayer. (4) GenLayer checks committed evidence and purpose; independent validation/Optimistic Democracy with appeals, no five-LLM/zero-hallucination guarantee. (5) X Layer reserves, rechecks and transfers exact permitted funds. (6) Value: auditable agent purchasing, budget isolation, recoverable workflows and human control, with CTA Create agent and View actual receipts.
Audio/animation describe an illustrative protocol flow, not live settlement. No fabricated identities, balances, transactions, usage totals or marketing percentages. Testnet labels remain explicit. Use existing real public explorer links for receipts. This uses TapeOut's unchanged NetlistVM and generated pinned circuit on X Layer testnet, not official mainnet CPU registry/DeWEB.
## Components and states
StoryFilm: autoplay-muted/playing/paused/muted/unmuted/loading/error/ended, play+pause, sound toggle, volume, replay, poster fallback. controls semantic buttons >=48px. Keyboard and touch equivalent.
StoryJourney: scroll-progress RAF with cleanup, sticky stage and path/chapter progress; semantic document headings and section anchors always readable. Screen readers receive chapter text, never per-frame live announcements. Responsive 320,390,768,1440,1920; avoid clipped text and horizontal overflow.
Reduced motion: poster, static stage and readable sections by default; no autoplay or forced scrolling. Allow deliberate film playback without enabling background scroll effects. Never infer user sound permission from time spent.
Loading/error states keep story copy visible, retry clear; no dependence on authentication. Sound does not continue on route change or tab hide.
