## Scene and composition
A persistent full-viewport canvas below existing header; foreground readable HTML sits asymmetrically and changes continuously with native scroll. The original terminal robot must be real 3D geometry, with rounded bevels, metallic soft reflections, joints/arms, expressive silver eyes and slot. ATM card: physically thin beveled engraved metal, original split-slot brand, chip/contact motif, subtle brushed surface. Procedural original card texture, no fake owner/amount/request counts. Studio lighting is part of the composition. Reflective highlights and contact shadows must make assets legible against graphite; do not ship flat black cubes.
Camera takes six acts: (0) agent and presented card hero; (1) reported costly mistake and owner's mandate, card close-up; (2) TapeOut mechanical gate/card tap, block branch stops before review; (3) evidence and independent validator evaluation with spatial depth; (4) exact X Layer settlement, vault to merchant; (5) receipt/human control/CTA. The same objects travel; no abrupt unrelated scene resets. Move pointer -> subtle camera/card response; touch equivalent using scroll. Character idle breathing/eyes is restrained and pauses offscreen/hidden/reduced-motion.

## Engineering interface and ownership

{progress:number (0..1), blocked:boolean, paused:boolean, reducedMotion:boolean, onReady?:()=>void, onError?:(message:string)=>void}.
