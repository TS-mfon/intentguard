# S01: Bind wallet challenges to their payment deployment

A signed testnet challenge copied into a mainnet store previously authorized a mainnet session. This is an isolation defense assuming challenge-record copying; no cross-database compromise was demonstrated.

Challenges now persist the payment environment and chain scope; login validates and atomically consumes that scope.

Regression: `tests/owner-challenge-security.test.ts`, including valid positive controls. Baseline source: `9d9eeb6`. Verification is local; deployed retests remain R17. No claim of zero vulnerabilities or third-party audit completion.
