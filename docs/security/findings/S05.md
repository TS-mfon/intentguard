# S05: Require exact delivery events before reporting PAID

A successful journal receipt without matching Paid/Transfer events was accepted as payment evidence. No fund-draining exploit was demonstrated.

Require the unique vault Paid event and configured-token Transfer matching immutable request, decision, merchant and amount, with confirmations in settlement and recovery.

Regression: `tests/payment-receipts.test.ts`, including valid positive controls. Baseline source: `9d9eeb6`. Verification is local; deployed retests remain R17. No claim of zero vulnerabilities or third-party audit completion.
